Where the ranking can change

Participation thresholds

For 38.4 < K ≤ 43.2 at ρR = 0, the farmer participates only under the fixed rule. The realized-profit comparison therefore reverses because the chosen-rule option has no trade.

When both rules trade

Under the baseline assumptions, the fixed rule generates more modeled completed units and farmer net earnings; the chosen rule generates more intermediary profit. These rankings depend on the assumed functional forms and parameters.

Use the coordination-cost slider to see the participation boundary. Use the ρR slider only for sensitivity scenarios until experimental estimates are available.

Planned test: separate reputation from price and quality perceptions

The proposal's 2 × 2 behavioral study varies (1) fixed versus intermediary-chosen margin rules and (2) sparse versus verified seller-history information. The household-facing listed price, product identity, quality evidence, quantity, and delivery stay the same across cells.

Measure household responses

Record purchase choice, trust, perceived price fairness, expected product quality, and willingness to pay. If the history signal changes perceived price or quality, analyze those as competing pathways rather than assigning the whole response to trust.

Connect the study to the model

Estimate the verified-history effect on purchase probability, translate it into the model's ρR demand shifter, then rerun the 2 × 2 comparison and coordination-cost sensitivity. Until then, ρR = 0 is the baseline.

Model scope. This browser app has no analytics, does not save inputs, and sends no responses to a server. It illustrates conditional predictions, not causal effects or empirical findings.
